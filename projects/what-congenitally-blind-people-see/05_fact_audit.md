## Causal integrity

- `Congenital lack of visual input → cortical reorganization` được trình bày là neuroplastic adaptation có support, không như một cơ chế duy nhất giải thích mọi kỹ năng.
- `Occipital activation → better performance` chỉ dùng khi study có correlation/task relationship phù hợp; không tuyên bố mọi activation tạo ưu thế hành vi.
- `Sight restoration → rapid cross-modal learning` được qualify theo participant/study, không thành universal biological law.
- Không dùng correlation để kết luận “mù làm thính giác tốt hơn”.

## Claims safe to keep

- Blindness is heterogeneous; congenital total blindness is a narrower case than the everyday word “blind”.
- Spatial representation and conceptual knowledge do not require a visual picture in the sighted sense.
- Color concepts can be learned through language and causal knowledge without color experience.
- Early/congenital blindness is associated with cross-modal recruitment of occipital cortex.
- Sensory compensation is selective and task-dependent, not a universal superpower.
- Sight-restoration evidence shows that receiving retinal input and knowing how to interpret it are not the same thing.
