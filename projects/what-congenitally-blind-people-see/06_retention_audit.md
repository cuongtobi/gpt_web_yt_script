## Act progression

### Act 1 — Black is not nothing
- Entry belief: blindness = black screen.
- Payoff: black is itself visual; “blind” is heterogeneous.
- Expansion: changes interpretation.
- Consequence: cần tìm model phi-thị-giác thay cho màn hình.

### Act 2 — A world without pictures can still have structure
- Entry question: không có hình ảnh thì biểu diễn vật thể/không gian thế nào?
- Payoff: spatial representation, conceptual color knowledge và dream evidence cho thấy representation ≠ visual qualia.
- Expansion: deepens mechanism + raises epistemic stakes.
- Consequence: nếu cognition vẫn phong phú, cortex được tổ chức ra sao?

### Act 3 — The “visual” brain changes jobs
- Entry question: occipital cortex làm gì khi không nhận visual input bình thường?
- Payoff: auditory/tactile/language recruitment; compensation is task-specific.
- Expansion: zoom out từ experience sang neural system.
- Consequence: nếu input xuất hiện muộn, não có “biết nhìn” ngay không?

### Act 4 — First sight exposes the hidden learning problem
- Entry belief: mở mắt + retinal signal = instantly understanding vision.
- Payoff: newly sighted participants initially struggle with cross-modal mapping, then can learn rapidly.
- Expansion: R4 changes model; perception is learned interpretation, not passive recording.
- Consequence: central question can now be answered without pretending access to another person’s qualia.

### Act 5 — Replace the wrong question
- Entry: viewer wants a literal internal picture.
- Payoff: no universal black field; rich nonvisual world representation; limits on what science can claim about qualia.
- Expansion: synthesis + ethical/epistemic reframe.

No `FLAT ACT` or `WEAK ACT TURN`.

## Reveal ladder

- R1: “Black” is not equivalent to absence of visual experience; blindness is a spectrum.
- R2: Space/object knowledge can exist without sighted-style mental pictures.
- R3: Color concepts can be rich without color qualia; dreams do not give a simple loophole.
- R4: Occipital cortex can support nonvisual tasks, and newly available visual input still has to be learned/interpreted.
- R5: The best answer is not an internal image but a distinction between sensory qualia, representation, and learned interpretation.

PASS: R4 changes the viewer’s model rather than merely adding drama.

## Top retention strengths

1. **The opening refutes its own visual metaphor.** This creates curiosity without an invented anecdote.
2. **Concrete-to-abstract rhythm.** Cup/room/color task/scan/Prakash repeatedly pull the story back from theory.
3. **Two independent reversals converge.** Cross-modal plasticity says “visual cortex” can do nonvisual work; sight restoration says visual input is not self-explanatory.
4. **Epistemic limit becomes payoff, not disclaimer.** The inability to directly access another person’s qualia strengthens the final reframe instead of weakening it.
