## Required changes

1. In the final, replace the phrase implying the Great Pyramid contains “hàng triệu khối đá” with a non-quantified description such as “một khối lượng đá khổng lồ”. The estimate is broadly familiar, but the exact number is not needed for the argument and was not independently re-verified in this run.
2. Keep the Colossus scrap-sale story explicitly qualified as a late/disputed tradition.
3. Keep the Statue of Zeus Constantinople/fire story explicitly uncertain.
4. Keep the Hanging Gardens at Nineveh as a debated hypothesis, not as the solution to the mystery.
5. Do not turn the synthesis “monuments survive only with social maintenance” into a universal law; present it as an explanatory pattern across the cases.

## Audit notes

No fabricated quote, probability, current statistic or unsupported exact historical dialogue is used. The draft avoids presenting disputed late traditions as settled fact. The central thesis is a synthesis of several independently supported case histories rather than a claim that every wonder shared one destruction mechanism.
