### H5 — Mechanism in motion

**Hook**

Domestication can happen one seed harvest at a time. Favor plants that make long, useful stalks and selection begins to reshape branching and fiber biology. Favor larger seeds and the crop shifts again. Favor female flowers with a particular cannabinoid chemistry and another pathway opens. Repeat those choices across many generations, and one flexible species can become crops that look as if they were designed for completely different jobs.

**Evidence anchors**
- C5, C6, C9, C17
- Ren et al. 2021; Dal Martello et al. 2024

**Bridge**
- Move from the general mechanism of artificial selection into the archaeological question: when do those different pressures first become visible?
