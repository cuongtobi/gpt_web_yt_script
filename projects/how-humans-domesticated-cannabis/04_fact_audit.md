## Required changes

No blocking factual changes remain. The draft already qualifies the three highest-risk historical claims. Final edit should preserve these exact protections:

1. Treat **~12,000 years ago** as a genomic/model-based early-Neolithic estimate, not an excavated first-domestication date.
2. Treat **~4,000 years ago** as an estimated population divergence between hemp- and drug-associated lineages, not a single global cultural split.
3. Describe the **Jirzankal** result as a residue profile consistent with relatively high-THC cannabis and strong evidence of psychoactive burning around 500 BCE; do not imply direct measurement of fresh ancient flower potency or a known modern-style breeding program.
