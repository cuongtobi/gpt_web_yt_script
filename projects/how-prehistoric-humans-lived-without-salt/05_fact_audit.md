- Nephron/hormone sequence is a verified mechanism visualization.
- Landscape montage (coast/rainforest/interior/salt spring) is explanatory visualization, explicitly not one specific group.

### Must stay generic
- Any hunter-gatherer meal visualization.

- Any transport/trade visualization at earliest Poiana layer.

No invented dialogue, emotion, motive, weather, smell, crowd behavior or exact ancient action appears in draft.

## Reconstruction boundary issues

1. “Một người có thể uống brine...” in the archaeology-preservation paragraph is plausible but reads slightly like specific behavior. **REWRITE** to “direct collection/use of brine can leave little durable evidence” without person-level invented action.
2. “Trước đó, spring chỉ là một điểm trong landscape” is rhetorical simplification. **REWRITE** to “same spring can take on greater economic importance as repeated demand grows.”
3. “Con người tìm được salt springs, học cách...” compresses a multi-regional, multi-millennial process. **QUALIFY** as cumulative history, not one sequence shared by all societies.
